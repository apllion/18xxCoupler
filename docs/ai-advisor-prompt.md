# 18xx AI Advisor — Prompt Template

Use this with any AI chat (Claude, ChatGPT, etc.) to get strategic advice for your 18xx game.

## How to use

1. Open your game in 18xxCoupler
2. Press **Ctrl+Shift+D** (or Cmd+Shift+D on Mac) — the game state is copied to your clipboard
3. Open a new AI chat
4. Paste the game state
5. Add your question below it

## Example prompt

Paste the game state (auto-generated by 18xxCoupler), then add:

---

I am playing as **[your player name]**. It is currently **[SR/OR]**.

**My situation:**
- [Describe what you're thinking about]
- [Any specific constraints or concerns]

**Questions:**
1. What should I do this turn?
2. Should I start a new corporation or invest in existing ones?
3. What train timing threats should I watch for?
4. How do I maximize my net worth from this position?

---

## Tips

- **Import from 18xx.games**: Load a game via the import feature, then Ctrl+Shift+D to get the state
- **Mid-game analysis**: The AI works best when you describe your strategic dilemma specifically
- **Train timing**: Always ask about train rusting — it's the most impactful decision driver
- **Multiple questions**: Ask 3-4 focused questions rather than one vague "what should I do"
- **Verify advice**: The AI may occasionally confuse title-specific rules despite the system prompt — always verify critical decisions against the rulebook
